Governance & Direction

Strategy facilitation, organisational risk management and performance management to help leadership teams establish direction and accountability.

GoldOurs works with governing boards and councils to formulate strategy, review performance areas, identify strategic drivers and set clear objectives.

Our governance support combines strategy, assessment of material risks and practical plans for managing performance.

How we can help

Strategy

Governing board or council strategy facilitation; review strategic documents and performance areas; identify strategic drivers; set key performance objectives.

Risk management

Identify strategic and operational risks; evaluate impact and likelihood; establish risk appetite; assess organisational risk maturity; software testing (full regression).

Performance management

Develop strategy and risk action plans, assign responsibilities and timelines, establish performance measures and agree monitoring intervals.
